About UTC and IST Time

What Is UTC Time?

UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) is the global time standard used worldwide.

What Is IST Time?

IST (Indian Standard Time) is the official time zone used across India and is UTC +5 hours 30 minutes.

Why Is UTC Used Worldwide?

UTC provides a single, consistent reference time that avoids confusion caused by time zone differences.

  • Standard reference for servers, APIs, and databases
  • Used in aviation, navigation, space, and military operations
  • Prevents daylight saving time errors
  • Ensures accurate logging and scheduling

UTC vs IST

UTC remains constant worldwide, while IST is India’s local time zone based on UTC +5:30.
